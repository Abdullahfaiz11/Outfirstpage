Our Philosophy

A Cozy Corner Born from a Love of True Craft

Co-founders Elena and Marcus began The Cozy Bean with an unhurried dream: to reintroduce Seattle to the restorative ritual of specialty coffee. Nestled in a leafy downtown pocket, our roastery bridges direct-trade mountain farms and your morning cup.

Every morning well before dawn, our deck oven warms up for fermented laminated viennoiserie, while Marcus selects single-origin microlots roasted in deliberate, small batches. We believe a coffee shop is not simply a stop along the commute, but a shared community living room.
