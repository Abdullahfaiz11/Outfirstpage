## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the tactile warmth, sensory richness, and unhurried craft of an independent artisanal cafe and roastery. It merges editorial elegance with warm hospitality, moving away from sterile corporate minimalist layouts in favor of an organic, inviting aesthetic.

### Aesthetic Movement & Philosophy
The visual direction fuses **Modern Editorial Warmth** with **Organic Tactility**:
- **Atmospheric Palette:** Earthy espresso tones, whipped cream backdrops, and muted botanical accents evocative of slow mornings, steam, and handcrafted ceramics.
- **Generous Spatial Breathing:** Plentiful margins and unhurried line-heights mimic the calming, quiet pause of a specialty coffee lounge.
- **Subtle Physicality:** Soft contours, organic card framing, delicate warm-tinted borders, and warm diffuse lighting give UI elements physical, touchable presence.
- **Target Audience:** Connoisseurs of craft coffee, neighborhood regulars, pastry lovers, and remote creatives seeking a calm, premium digital home for menus, table booking, bean subscriptions, and cafe storytelling.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from the sensory nuances of specialty roasting, steamed milk, and botanical cafe interiors. The design strictly avoids cool gray tones or pure blacks (`#000000`), anchoring all neutrals in warm milk and linen hues.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#3E2B20` / Deep Espresso):** The core grounding shade. Used for high-emphasis headlines, strong brand signatures, and high-priority call-to-action buttons. Deep variant (`#2B1E16`) serves as the dominant high-contrast text color on cream surfaces.
- **Secondary (`#6F4E37` / Rich Mocha):** Mid-tone roast used for secondary buttons, active nav elements, category markers, and subtle decorative accents.
- **Tertiary (`#5F7D61` / Earthy Sage):** Botanical balance derived from fresh coffee plants and cafe greenery. Applied to badges, botanical tags, success states, and dietary indicators (vegan, gluten-free, organic). Lighter accent tint (`#7A9A7B`) serves for gentle highlights and hover states.
- **Amber Accent (`#D98E32` / Golden Honey):** Reserved for star ratings, seasonal flags, bakery badges, and micro-delight moments.
- **Backgrounds & Neutrals:**
  - Base canvas (`#FDF8F2` / Warm Cream): Primary canvas background mimicking fine vellum and frothy milk.
  - Surface Container (`#F7EFE5` / Soft Oat): Secondary background for cards, hero containers, and elevated sheets.
  - Surface Border / Divider (`#EFE2D3` / Steamed Crema): Tactile, ultra-low contrast line dividers.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the editorial romance of **Playfair Display** with the approachable clarity of **Plus Jakarta Sans**. 

### Hierarchy Guidelines
- **Headlines (Playfair Display):** Imparts heritage, care, and bespoke craft. High-tier headings feature tight letter-spacing and moderate line heights to mimic high-end culinary publications. Italicized flourishes may be applied selectively to highlight single origin regions or seasonal words.
- **Body & Controls (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Balances the serif's ornate character with open counters and humanist terminal curves, providing effortless legibility for ingredient lists, tasting notes, prices, and interactive forms.
- **Label & Badges:** `label-caps` must always be rendered in uppercase with wide tracking for metadata tags (e.g., "WASHED PROCESS", "COLOMBIA HUILA", "GLUTEN-FREE").

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy follows a relaxed, editorial rhythm that avoids claustrophobic, data-dense arrangements. Space is an active design element, functioning as the digital equivalent of peaceful cafe seating.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column fluid grid contained within a max-width of `1280px`. Margins are set to `margin` (3rem) with `gutter` (1.5rem). Used for alternating asymmetrical product highlights and multi-column menu layouts.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column grid with `2rem` margins and `1.25rem` gutters. Cards reflow into paired 2-column modules.
- **Mobile (320px – 767px):** 4-column grid with `margin-mobile` (1.25rem) and `gutter-mobile` (1rem). Dense multi-columns collapse cleanly into stacked editorial cards or horizontal kinetic carousels.

### Component Spacing Rhythm
- `space-xs` (4px) and `space-sm` (8px): Micro-gaps between badge icons, price sub-labels, and input icon padding.
- `space-md` (16px): Internal cell padding for compact cards, button padding, and list item spacing.
- `space-lg` (28px): Standard internal padding for beverage cards, tasting note blocks, and modal sheets.
- `space-xl` (40px): Section breaks between menu categories and testimonial quotes.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids high-contrast synthetic dropshadows. Depth is articulated through warm tonal layering and soft ambient light filtered through warm room air.

### Depth Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Flat Ground):** Base canvas (`#FDF8F2`).
- **Level 1 (Surface Containers):** Layered cards and panels rendered in `#F7EFE5` with a warm border rule (`1px solid #EFE2D3`). Shadows are entirely absent or whisper-light: `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(62, 43, 32, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating Cards & Dropdowns):** Subtle ambient depth tinted with roasted espresso: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(62, 43, 32, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(62, 43, 32, 0.04)`. Accompanied by `#EFE2D3` edge definition.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Toast Notifications):** Gentle floating presence: `0 20px 40px -8px rgba(43, 30, 22, 0.12), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(43, 30, 22, 0.06)`. Backdrops use a warm espresso wash: `rgba(43, 30, 22, 0.45)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(8px)`.

## Shapes

The shape system communicates softness, approachable craft, and ceramic-inspired geometry. Straight edges are tempered by tactile radii reminiscent of smooth cafe tables and pottery.

### Shape Language & Curvature Tokens
- **Standard UI Containers (Cards, Inputs, Modals):** Inherit `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / 16px) or `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem` / 24px), providing friendly, soft-cornered frames.
- **Interactive Buttons & Badges:** Utilize full pill forms (`rounded-full` / `9999px`) to create smooth, touchable surfaces that contrast gracefully against the structural grid.
- **Artisanal Image Framing:** Photography depicting coffee pours, pastries, and shop scenes uses asymmetrical soft radii (e.g., top-left and bottom-right `2rem`, top-right and bottom-left `1rem`) or a classic rounded arch to echo traditional cafe architecture.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Espresso Roast (`#3E2B20`) background with Warm Foamy Cream (`#FDF8F2`) typography. Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padded `14px 28px`. Hover shifts to `#2B1E16` with a gentle lift (`translateY(-1px)`).
- **Secondary / Outline:** Rich Mocha border (`1.5px solid #6F4E37`) with Mocha typography on transparent or `#FDF8F2` background. Hover fills surface with `#F7EFE5`.
- **Subtle / Ghost:** Pure text with `label-lg` styling in `#3E2B20`, underlined with a delicate `#D98E32` warm amber indicator on hover.

### Chips & Badges
- **Pill Badges:** Capsule form (`rounded-full`), padding `4px 12px`.
- **Dietary & Botanical Chips:** Earthy Sage tint (`background: rgba(95, 125, 97, 0.12)`, `color: #5F7D61`).
- **Roast Profile & Seasonal Chips:** Golden Amber tint (`background: rgba(217, 142, 50, 0.12)`, `color: #9E641B`).
- **Subtle Category Pills:** `#F7EFE5` background with `#6F4E37` typography and `#EFE2D3` border.

### Cards (Menu Items, Bakery, Subscriptions)
- **Signature Drink & Bakery Cards:** Background `#F7EFE5`, border `1px solid #EFE2D3`, rounded `1.25rem`. Integrated photography sits at the top with an internal inset padding of `8px` or a flush `rounded-t-[1.25rem]`. Pricing is displayed in clean `Plus Jakarta Sans` bold, while item names take `Playfair Display`.
- **Testimonial Cards:** Background `#FDF8F2` with subtle ambient shadow. Features an oversized decorative quotation mark in `#D98E32` at `20%` opacity, customer name in `label-lg`, and quote in `body-md` italic.

### Form Inputs & Checkboxes
- **Text Inputs:** Soft Oat `#F7EFE5` field background with `#EFE2D3` border, rounded `0.75rem`. Text in `#2B1E16`, placeholder in `#6F4E37` at 50% opacity. Focused state transitions border to `#3E2B20` with a warm halo ring (`0 0 0 3px rgba(62, 43, 32, 0.1)`).
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Rounded `6px` (checkbox) and full circle (radio). Border `1.5px solid #6F4E37`. Checked state fills with `#3E2B20` featuring a cream checkmark icon.

### Lists & Dividers
- **Menu Lists:** Split row design with item title on the left and dotted or dashed leader line in `#EFE2D3` connecting to the price on the right. Subtitle tasting notes placed in `body-sm` beneath.
- **Section Dividers:** 1px stroke in `#EFE2D3`, occasionally accented by a centered floral or coffee-bean glyph in `#6F4E37`.
