# The Cozy Bean - Artisanal Roasters & Kitchen ☕

A modern, responsive, and artisanal landing page for **The Cozy Bean**, a boutique coffee shop and bakery. Crafted with rich earthy aesthetics, modern typography, and smooth interactive features.

---

## 🌟 Live Demo
Once GitHub Pages is activated, your website will be live at:

---

## ✨ Features
- **Modern Artisanal Design:** Tailored color palette, warm typography (Playfair Display & Plus Jakarta Sans), and smooth animations.
- **Interactive Menu:** Filterable coffee and bakery menu with pricing and detailed flavor notes.
- **Interactive Order Modal / Cart:** Working order bag with dynamic counter and slide-out checkout.
- **Account Drawer:** Sign in / Sign up slide-over panel.
- **Visual Gallery & Atmosphere:** High-resolution cafe imagery and photo showcases.
- **Customer Reviews:** Real-time rating highlights and customer testimonials.
- **Visit Us & Location:** Business hours, interactive map placeholder, and directions.
- **100% Responsive:** Seamlessly optimized for mobile, tablet, and desktop screens.
